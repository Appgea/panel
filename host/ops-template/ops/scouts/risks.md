---
title: What expires or changes soon
who: the owner three months from now. Evidence: certificates, SDK floors, store policy pages and law changes, each with a date.
every: routine
agent: claude
---

Lens: certificates and keys that expire, SDK and policy deadlines, law changes in the markets you sell in.
Each with a date becomes a finding with a `Recheck:` date when it is not urgent yet.

1. The mechanical half: signing certificates and profiles, server TLS, the Android target SDK against the Play
   floor, and any dates you keep. Script it once and run the script each time.
2. The web half, three reads:
   - https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/, newest entry first.
   - https://play.google/developer-content-policy/, the newest policy announcement.
   - One search for law that touches apps or stores in each market you sell in.
   A date that keeps coming back belongs in your script, not here, so the next run reads it off the script.

A finding: a dated change that hits an app, with the date, the app and the one step that meets it.
Not a finding: a rule already met, a rumour with no date.
