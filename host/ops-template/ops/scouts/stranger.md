---
title: Does a stranger find the app and install it?
who: a person who has not installed the app yet. Evidence: the store page, the store's numbers by country, and the links that send people to it.
store: live
every: routine
agent: claude
---

Lens: the app from outside, before the install. What a stranger sees on the store page, in which language, and what
sends them there. One app a run. Read only: never change a store listing.
After the install is the `analytics` lens.

Abilities, in `scouts/abilities/`:
- `store-page`: the name, the first lines and the first screenshots, held against the page's own tap and install rates.
- `locale-reach`: the countries that see the page, against the languages of the page, the app and the paywall.
- `house-ad`: an app with users and no income, and whether it sends them to a sibling app that earns.
- `share-loop`: every share and invite in the code, and whether what it sends can bring the next user.

Yours to add when all of them ran on the app: what else decides an install. For example, a preview video that shows
menus before play, a rating too low to pass a search list, an app website no search finds, or a custom store page
no campaign uses.

A finding: a concrete weak point a stranger meets before the install, with the page text, the number or the
`path:line` that shows it. A fix that changes store text or screenshots is a draft: the release puts it live.
Not a finding: a taste call on art, a keyword guess with no number, a country with under 100 impressions a month.
