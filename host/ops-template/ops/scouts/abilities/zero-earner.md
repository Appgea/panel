Lens: an app that earns little or nothing, walked as a chain from launch to a finished purchase. The first link that cannot work is the finding. Read only.

1. The run names the app. Write down what it sells, live: the products and offerings in the store and the purchase backend.
2. Walk the chain in the code. At each link, name the `path:line` and what it needs to be true: the product ids load, the paywall is reachable, the buy button calls the store, the result grants access, the gate reads that grant.
3. Stop at the first broken link. One finding, the first.

A finding: the first link that cannot work, or no feature gated behind the purchase, with every `path:line` of the walk.
Not a finding: a chain that completes in an app with few users, which is a `stranger` matter.
