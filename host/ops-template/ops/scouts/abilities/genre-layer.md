Lens: a genre veteran mid-game, looking at one system's choices. Read only.

1. Pick one system the game has: combat, economy, diplomacy, progression, or the like.
2. List its rules files and read the biggest two.
3. Write down each choice the player has in it: each order or setting the rules accept, with what it costs and what it feeds.
4. Write down the same system in two or three of the best games of the genre, as a veteran knows it: its choices, costs and feeds.
5. Compare. Name the one layer they have and this game lacks.

A finding: the one missing layer: the new choice, its cost, what it feeds, and the genre game that does it.
Not a finding: a cosmetic reward, a whole-system redesign, a layer the game's design notes reject.
