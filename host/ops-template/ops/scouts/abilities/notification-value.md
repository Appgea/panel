Lens: every reminder and push, from the permission request through delivery and the tap. Read only.

1. The run names the app. Pull its repo.
2. Find scheduling and permission calls.
3. Find copy, cancellation and tap handling.
4. Map each reminder to its text, trigger, payload and destination. Follow what cancels it when the task is done.
5. Follow taps on cold and warm start to the action, with the right item loaded.

A finding: a reminder that still fires after its task is done, a tap that loses the action, or a permission asked before any value.
Not a finding: an absent notification feature, an intentional recurring reminder.
