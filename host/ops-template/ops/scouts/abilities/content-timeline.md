Lens: a genre veteran on a long campaign, waiting for something to happen. Read only.

1. Find the dated or scripted content: events, decisions, quests.
2. Count it by period and by playable side.
3. Hold the counts against the campaign's length and the sides the player can pick.

A finding: one stretch or one side with little or nothing scripted, with five to ten pieces of content that fit it.
Not a finding: a stretch the design leaves quiet on purpose.
