Lens: one app in each country that already looks at it: the page, the app and the paywall, each in that language. Read only.

1. The run names the app. List its store languages.
2. Find its top 5 countries by impressions.
3. For each, check: page text, screenshots, app strings, paywall strings in that language.

A finding: a country in the top 5 by impressions with a missing piece, ranked by impressions.
Not a finding: a country under 100 impressions a month, a dialect of a language already covered.
