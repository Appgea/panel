Lens: a veteran of this genre, in the middle of a single match, round or score run. Read only.

1. The run names the app. Find its game loop files.
2. List what the player decides in one match: moves, bets, risks, power-ups, undo.
3. List what the computer opponent decides, when there is one, and read its pick function.
4. Answer yes or no with a `path:line`: is there a risky choice, a comeback, a reason to start the next match?

A finding: one missing or shallow rule, what the player would decide with it, what it costs, and the genre game that has it.
Not a finding: a cosmetic reward, a store or price idea, a bug in a rule that exists.
