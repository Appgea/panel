Lens: the parts of the app as a list, and the road to each one. A part nobody can find is a part nobody uses. Read only.

1. The run names the app. List each main part or feature from `apps/<key>.md` and the README.
2. List each benefit line on the paywall.
3. For each part and promise, find its screen and its callers. Count the taps from the home screen.
4. For each promise, check the gate: is it really locked for a free user?
5. Fill one row per part: doors, taps, gated yes or no, measured yes or no.

A finding: a promised feature with no door, a part more than 3 taps deep with no other way in, or a promise that is not gated.
Not a finding: a settings page 3 taps deep, a debug screen, a part parked on purpose.
