Lens: the screen after each way a purchase can end: success, cancel, pending, failed, already owned. Read only.

1. Find the purchase call and every branch of its result.
2. For each branch, follow it to what the screen shows.
3. Check the pending and already-owned cases most of all.

A finding: a branch that shows success on failure, failure on success, or nothing, at `path:line`.
Not a finding: a message wording preference.
