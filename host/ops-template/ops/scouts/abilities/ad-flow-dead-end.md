Lens: an ad that fails, loads late or is dismissed, followed to the reward and the next screen. Read only.

1. Find every ad show call: interstitial, rewarded, app open.
2. For each, follow the no-fill, the late load, the dismissal and the error path.
3. Check the reward: given once, only on completion, and the screen moves on.

A finding: an ad path that strands the user, gives no reward after a full view, or rewards twice, at `path:line`.
Not a finding: a fill rate number, an ad placement taste call.
