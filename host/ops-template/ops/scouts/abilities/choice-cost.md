Lens: a genre veteran weighing one system's choices. Read only.

1. Pick one system. List its choices in the data files: costs, upkeep, requirements, cooldowns, exclusions.
2. For each choice, find what it takes: a resource, time, a slot, or another option it closes.
3. Answer with a `path:line`: is there a state where skipping this choice is the right call?

A finding: a choice with no cost and no state where skipping it is right, with the cost to add and its value.
Not a finding: a free tutorial step, a choice whose cost lives in another system you traced.
