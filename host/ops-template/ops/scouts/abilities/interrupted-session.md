title: Can someone resume a task after an interruption?
family: journey
agent: claude
---

Lens: a user mid-task who presses Back, dismisses a sheet or leaves the app. Read only.

1. Choose one flow in the named app where someone has already entered data or earned progress.
2. Follow the visible action and the platform gesture from the same state. Do both reach the same result?
3. Trace background and foreground on that flow. If the app promises recovery after a cold start, check the saved form and its reader too.

A finding: a reachable interruption that discards committed progress, silently drops a draft against the visible action, or applies an action twice.
Not a finding: a confirmed discard, a flow with nothing to lose.
