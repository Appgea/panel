Lens: entitlement lifetime across devices, reinstalls and billing changes, beyond the successful purchase. Read only.

1. The run names the app. Pull its repo.
2. Find the entitlement sources: restore calls, customer info, current entitlements.
3. Find identity, expiry and caches.
4. Trace a second device and a reinstall from empty local storage through restore to every paid gate.
5. Trace a refund, a billing grace period and an offline launch from the last known entitlement.

A finding: a buyer who loses access on restore, or access that contradicts the verified entitlement.
Not a finding: unsupported family sharing, an offline cache within its stated lifetime.
