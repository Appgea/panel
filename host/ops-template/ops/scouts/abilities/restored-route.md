---
title: Can the app reopen when its saved destination has disappeared?
family: journey
agent: claude
---

Lens: a user whose app reopens on its own to a screen whose item was deleted or expired. Read only.

1. Find where the app saves and restores the last route or the last opened item.
2. Follow a restore when that item no longer exists.
3. Check what the screen does: a crash, an empty screen, or a sensible fallback.

A finding: a restore that crashes or strands the user, with the save and restore `path:line`.
Not a finding: an app that never restores a route.
