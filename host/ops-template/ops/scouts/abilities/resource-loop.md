Lens: a genre veteran reading the stockpiles. Read only.

1. List the resources the player sees on screen.
2. For each, find where the rules add it and where they take it.
3. Answer with a `path:line`: does it have a source after the start, a sink, and a player choice about where it goes?

A finding: a resource with no sink, no source after the start, or no player choice where it goes, with the loop to add.
Not a finding: a score or a counter that is only shown, not spent, by design.
