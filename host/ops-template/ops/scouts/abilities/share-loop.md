Lens: what leaves the app when a user shares, and whether a stranger who gets it can find the app. Read only.

1. The run names the app. Find every share call in the code.
2. For each, read what it sends: the text, the image and the link.
3. Check: does it name the app, does the link work, does it open the store or the app?
4. Name the app's happy moments: a win, a finished task, a streak day. Is there a share there?

A finding: a share that sends no name or no working link, or the app's best moment with no share at all.
Not a finding: an app with nothing a user would show another person, a share of private data.
